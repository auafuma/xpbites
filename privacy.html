Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Who we are

XP Bites makes short gaming news videos. XP Bites Publisher is our own internal tool. It posts our videos to the XP Bites accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram after a person on our team has reviewed and approved each one. It only connects to accounts XP Bites owns, and nobody outside XP Bites can sign in to it.

This website

This website has no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

YouTube

XP Bites Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy.

When we sign in with Google and approve access, the tool receives an access token and a refresh token for our channel. With them it:

  • uploads videos our team has approved, with the title, description, release time and AI-content label we chose, and changes a release time when we ask;
  • reads our channel's details, our videos and their public statistics, and our channel's YouTube Analytics and Reporting data (views, watch time, retention, impressions and click-through rate);
  • reads the public comments on our videos, including each commenter's display name, channel ID and comment text, so we can answer questions and remove spam;
  • reads the caption tracks of our own videos;
  • marks clear spam comments on our videos as rejected. Only a person on our team ever bans a commenter.

You can remove the tool's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

TikTok

When we connect our TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool:

  • an access token and a refresh token, so it can post our approved videos;
  • the account's open ID and basic profile information (username, display name and avatar), so we can confirm which account we are posting to.

The tool uses these only to post XP Bites videos and check their posting status. It does not read data about viewers or other TikTok users. You can remove access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions.

Instagram

When we connect our Instagram professional account through Meta, the tool receives:

  • an access token, so it can publish our approved videos as Reels;
  • the account's Instagram ID, username and account type, so we can confirm which account we are posting to;
  • the IDs and publishing status of the posts it creates.

The tool uses these only to publish XP Bites videos and check that they went live. It does not read data about followers, viewers or other Instagram users. Meta handles data under the Meta Privacy Policy. You can remove access in Instagram under Settings and activity, then Website permissions, then Apps and websites.

How we use this data

Only to run the XP Bites accounts: posting our own videos, seeing how they performed, and handling comments on them. We do not use any of it for advertising. We do not build profiles of viewers or commenters. We never sell it.

Who we share it with

XP Bites Publisher uses Anthropic's Claude AI service to summarise how our videos performed and to sort comments on our YouTube videos. To do that, it sends our channel's statistics and the text of those comments to Anthropic, which processes them under Anthropic's Privacy Policy. We share data with no one else, and nothing is posted as a reply unless a person on our team writes or approves it.

Where it is stored and for how long

  • Access tokens, and any data the tool keeps, are stored only on the XP Bites team's own computer. Only our own Windows user account can read those files. Nothing is stored on a public server.
  • Commenter names and comment text are deleted within 30 days.
  • Statistics about our own videos are kept while our account stays connected.
  • If we disconnect an account, or access is revoked, we delete the data the tool stored for that platform within 30 days.

Deleting your data

If you commented on one of our videos, or think we hold data about you, email auafuma@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your username on the platform. We will delete everything we hold about you within 30 days and reply to confirm. Deleting a token file on our computer also ends the tool's access to that platform immediately.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Questions about privacy: auafuma@gmail.com.
