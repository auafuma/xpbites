XP Bites

Fast breakdowns of major gaming news.

XP Bites makes short gaming news videos for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. XP Bites Publisher is our own tool: after a person on our team reviews and approves each video, it posts that video to the XP Bites accounts.

XP Bites Publisher uses YouTube API Services. It uploads our approved videos to our own YouTube channel, @XPbitesNews, sets their release time, and reads that channel's comments and statistics.
