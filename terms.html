What this covers

These terms cover the XP Bites website and XP Bites Publisher. XP Bites Publisher is the tool XP Bites uses to post its own videos to its own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Who can use XP Bites Publisher

XP Bites Publisher is an internal tool. Only the XP Bites team uses it, and only to post to accounts XP Bites owns. It is not offered to the public, and nobody outside XP Bites can sign in to it.

How posting works

  • A person on the XP Bites team reviews every video and approves it before anything is posted. On YouTube, they also choose the release date and time.
  • Each video is posted with the title, caption, privacy setting and interaction settings the team chose.
  • Videos made with AI narration or AI-generated images are labelled as AI-generated content on every platform.
  • Replies to comments are only ever posted by a person on our team. The tool only removes clear spam on its own.

Platform terms

Our use of each platform also follows that platform's own rules:

  • YouTube: the YouTube Community Guidelines and the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • TikTok: the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Community Guidelines.
  • Instagram: the Instagram Terms of Use, the Instagram Community Guidelines and the Meta Platform Terms.
