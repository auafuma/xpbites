What this covers

These terms cover the XP Bites website and XP Bites Publisher, the tool XP Bites uses to post its own videos to its own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Who can use XP Bites Publisher

XP Bites Publisher is an internal tool. Only the XP Bites team uses it, and only to post to accounts XP Bites owns. It is not offered to the public, and nobody outside XP Bites can sign in to it.

How posting works

  • A person on the XP Bites team reviews every video and approves it before anything is posted.
  • Videos are posted with the caption, privacy setting and interaction settings the team chose.
  • Videos follow the TikTok Community Guidelines, the TikTok Terms of Service, the YouTube Community Guidelines and the YouTube Terms of Service. Videos made with AI narration or generated images are labelled as AI-generated content.
